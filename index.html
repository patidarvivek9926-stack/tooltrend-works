How to add affiliate links on this site

Replace the placeholder links with your real affiliate URLs from Amazon, Impact, PartnerStack, Digistore24, Admitad, Cuelinks, or direct SaaS partner programs.

Join affiliate programs

Pick 5 to 10 good programs in one niche. For this site, software and AI tools are better than random products because they are easier to compare and often have better commission structures.

Replace button links

In the code, buttons currently use href="#" or section links. Change those to your affiliate URLs like https://partner-link.com/offer123.

Add disclosures clearly

Keep a visible affiliate disclosure near the top, on review pages, and inside the footer or policy pages. Do not hide it.

Example affiliate button code

Paste your affiliate URL in place of the example link below.

<a href="https://your-affiliate-link.com/tool-offer" class="btn btn-primary" target="_blank" rel="sponsored nofollow">Try This Tool</a>
Use rel="sponsored nofollow" on affiliate links. Keep buttons honest like “View pricing”, “Try free”, or “See official site”.

Where affiliate links work best

  • Top 10 roundup pages
  • Tool comparison pages like A vs B
  • Best tools for a use-case page
  • Pricing and alternatives articles
  • Dedicated review pages with pros/cons
Best practice: mix affiliate links with real information, screenshots, pros/cons, and practical use-cases. Thin pages convert worse and may rank worse.

Why is this website niche better than random trending news?+
Because software and AI tool queries are easier to monetize with affiliate links, easier to update, and safer for long-term SEO than chasing one-day trends with thin content.
Can I use this website on GitHub Pages with my .works domain?+
Yes. This is a static HTML website, so you can upload it to a GitHub repository, enable GitHub Pages, and then connect your .works domain in the Pages custom-domain settings and your DNS panel.
How many articles should I publish first?+
Start with 10 to 20 strong pages: 3 category pages, 5 roundup posts, 5 comparison posts, and a few use-case guides. Internal linking matters a lot.
Can I add AdSense later too?+
Yes, but do not clutter the pages. If the site gets traffic, you can combine AdSense with affiliate links and email capture.
